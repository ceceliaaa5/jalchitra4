# Demo guide (about 3 minutes)
1. **Problem (30s):** wetlands change size with the seasons; static maps go stale and departments hold different versions.
2. **Season switch (45s):** click Winter, then Monsoon. Wetlands grow and shrink; point to the total and the bar chart.
3. **Wetland detail (45s):** click Sone Beel. Show peak vs low extent, irrigated area, fish potential and the advisory. Switch to Winter to see the advisory change to low water.
4. **Table and layers (20s):** click a table row; toggle canals and fishery sites.
5. **How it goes live (40s):** show the architecture page of the PDF: Sentinel-1 radar for cloud cover, water masks, PostGIS, API.
**Be upfront:** data is sample data and the satellite pipeline is proposed, not built. Judges respond well to that honesty.
**Likely questions:** accuracy (field validation in the pilot district), data source (free Sentinel), cost (one VM, no licences).
